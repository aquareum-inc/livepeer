# Design proposals

Decisions made beyond the Livepeer brand anchors, kept as a running list so they can be proposed back as an
update to Livepeer's design guidelines. Anchors themselves (semantic tokens, green only for non-interactive
brand expression, Inter + Geist Mono, mono logo, 4px rhythm) are unchanged. Screenshots are in
`docs/screenshots/`; they were taken in mock-only mode at 1440×900 and 375×812.

| 23 | **The agent's model on Home and Chat; capabilities in the studio.** Following the reference's agentic chat, the composer's menu picks the language model that runs the agent (Auto, then the models on the network, each with a line on what sets it apart and Busy when no runner is free), never a capability. The menu lists models, not deployments: apps serving the same model share one row that sends to the copy with a runner free and the most context. Who runs a model is never shown. With fewer than two models the menu is hidden, since Auto is the only real choice. | 25 | **Plan and credits carries colour where it means something.** The balance card shows the budget as a slim meter, the remaining share in brand green, with "left" and "spent" beneath instead of a sentence of figures. A card’s light brightens while its button is hovered or focused, not the whole card, since only the button acts; one hue throughout, because tiers are steps on one ladder and brightness says "more" better than colour. Check marks are brand green at 75%. Buttons stay neutral: green is expression, never a control. The plans sit three across only when the section is wide enough for each price on one line (a container query), and stack otherwise, never two and one. Premium steps up in the material of each card, not in a badge: the first plan is the plain card, the middle one a lifted surface (`--surface-lifted`) with a green-only glow at its top and an edge that starts green and fades, and the top one lit glass, an aurora field in brand green, teal and a trace of violet (`--aurora-teal`, `--aurora-violet`) frosted under blur, inside an edge that runs from green into teal, with a sheen that crosses it while its button is hovered (off with reduced motion). Tints scale by `--aurora-strength`, gentler on the light theme. On the light theme the fields and the top-edge glow are replaced by one even wash across the top of the card (green into teal on the top plan, green on the middle), fading down: on white, soft fields read as smudges. A surface lift alone didn't separate the first two plans; the middle plan needs a quieter share of the same light. The materials are Home's, so the top plan feels like the product at its fullest; they follow position, so they suit any operator's plans. Plans are told apart by what differs, not by icons: the allowance as a second headline with a bar against the largest plan, and a list of only what the plan adds ("Everything in Creator, plus:"). A small tier mark beside each name was tried as signal bars, stacked layers and concentric rings and dropped: it only restated rank, which the order, the light and the allowance already show.
